### On Quasiparticles within the Refined Gribov-Zwanziger Model

- Source: [arXiv:2609.04423v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04423v1); Garcia, Felipe F., Capri, Marcio A. L., Mintz, Bruno W..
- Dates: first 2026-09-03; latest 2026-09-03. **new-in-trailing-week**.
- Evidence: **literature / preprint**. Reviewed: Abstract and submission history.
- Reported result: The refined Gribov-Zwanziger effective model is reexamined through PT symmetry, with a proposed interpretation of quadratic excitations in the real-mass-pole case.
- Limits: An effective gauge-fixed propagator and real poles do not establish reflection positivity for gauge-invariant Wilson observables, OS reconstruction, or a physical mass gap.

### Depth-1 expanders on the unitary group and applications

- Source: [arXiv:2609.01605v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01605v1); Anshu, Anurag, Balasubramanian, Shankar, Haferkamp, Jonas, Harrow, Aram W., Tan, Xinyu.
- Dates: first 2026-09-01; latest 2026-09-01. **background**.
- Evidence: **literature / preprint**. Reviewed: Abstract and submission history.
- Reported result: Anshu and collaborators construct constant-degree, constant-gap quantum expanders with depth-one local circuit realizations and apply them to one-dimensional Hamiltonians with an entanglement-gap relation.
- Limits: An expander gap, a transfer gap and a many-body Hamiltonian gap are different operators. Track dimension, locality and the definition of gap before translating. First posted 1 September.

### Efficient Quantum Simulations of Yang-Mills theory with Maximal-tree Gauge

- Source: [arXiv:2608.27267v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27267v1); Li, Tianyin, Li, Ying-Ying, Wang, Xiaoyang, Xing, Hongxi.
- Dates: first 2026-08-27; latest 2026-08-27. **background**.
- Evidence: **literature / preprint**. Reviewed: v1: abstract and maximal-tree gauge construction.
- Reported result: Li and collaborators use maximal-tree gauge and field-amplitude digitization to obtain polynomial resource bounds for Hamiltonian Yang-Mills simulation through QSVT.
- Limits: Hamiltonian simulation complexity is distinct from an exact Haar edge-coordinate equivalence, Euclidean reflection positivity, and a continuum mass gap. First posted 27 August; a September news mention is not a new submission.
